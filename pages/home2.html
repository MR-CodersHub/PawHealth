Clinical Care Protocol

The PawHealth Clinical Care Journey

We remove the stress and mystery of veterinary hospital visits through a transparent, 5-stage clinical workflow where you are an active, fully informed partner every step of the way.

01 Minute 0–5

Fear-Free Reception & Low-Stress Triage

Immediate escort into a quiet species-separated exam suite. Gentle vital signs check (weight, pulse, respiratory rate) accompanied by calming treats and scent-calmed towels.

02 Minute 5–20

Unhurried 12-System Clinical Examination

A comprehensive head-to-tail physical exam with pet parents present. Thorough auscultation of heart/lungs, ophthalmic inspection, dental grade scoring, abdominal palpation, and orthopedic check.

03 Minute 20–35

Point-of-Care Diagnostics & Shared Review

When indicated, on-site IDEXX bloodwork and digital radiographs are processed in under 15 minutes. Results are displayed on in-room monitors and thoroughly explained in plain language.

04 Minute 35–45

Collaborative Plan & Upfront Itemized Estimate

Zero surprises. You receive a transparent, printed treatment estimate detailing every medication, procedure, and follow-up option before any treatment or diagnostic begins.

05 Post-Visit Follow-Up

Monitored Recovery & Dedicated Nurse Check-In

Clear digital take-home instructions, medications with custom dosing guides, and a personal check-in SMS or phone call from your veterinary technician within 24–48 hours.
