Why Immunization Matters

The Foundation of Lifelong Immunity

Vaccines stimulate your pet's immune system to produce protective antibodies against deadly infectious diseases like Parvovirus, Distemper, Feline Leukemia, and Rabies. Without vaccination, an animal exposed to these pathogens faces severe clinical illness, high hospitalization costs, and tragic mortality.

At PawHealth, we distinguish between Core Vaccines (essential for every companion animal regardless of lifestyle) and Lifestyle Vaccines (administered only when your pet's environment, boarding frequency, or geographic risks warrant them).

What Every Vaccination Visit Includes

We never just administer an injection. Every vaccination appointment is a comprehensive health checkup.

Complete Physical Exam

Pre-vaccine vitals, weight check, temperature, heart auscultation, and lymph node check to verify pet is 100% healthy before vaccination.

Medical History Review

Reviewing past vaccines, adverse reactions, recent lifestyle shifts, boarding plans, and travel itineraries.

Tailored Vaccine Protocol

Administering only the specific vaccines suitable for your pet's age, species, and risk profile using ultra-fine, gentle needles.

Digital Portal Sync

Immediate synchronization to your Pet Owner Dashboard with batch numbers, dates, and printable medical certificates.

Automated Due Alerts

Email and SMS reminder schedules so you never accidentally lapse on an essential booster.

Post-Care Guidance

Written follow-up instructions on normal mild sleepiness versus what requires urgent medical attention.
