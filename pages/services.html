Internal Medicine & Point-of-Care Diagnostics

When a pet is ill, minutes matter. Our in-house IDEXX clinical laboratory and low-radiation digital imaging suites produce immediate, definitive data so targeted medical therapy begins without delay.

In-House Point-of-Care IDEXX Laboratory

Complete blood counts (CBC), comprehensive chemistry panels, electrolytes, blood gas, and in-clinic cytology in under 15 minutes.
