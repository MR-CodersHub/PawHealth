Foundation of Longevity

Preventive Healthcare & Wellness

Preventive care is the most compassionate and cost-effective approach to veterinary medicine. By detecting subtle biological shifts before clinical symptoms arise, we help pets live happier, healthier, and longer lives.

  • ✓
    Routine Physical Examinations: Nose-to-tail check of eyes, ears, heart, lungs, lymph nodes, and musculoskeletal condition.
  • ✓
    Customized Immunizations: Core vaccines (Rabies, DHPP, FVRCP) plus lifestyle options tailored to local risks.
  • ✓
    Parasite Screening & Prevention: Year-round oral and topical controls for fleas, ticks, heartworm, and intestinal worms.
  • ✓
    Life-Stage Checkups: Dedicated developmental reviews for puppies & kittens and biannual reviews for senior companions.

Precision Diagnostics

Internal Medicine & Diagnostic Services

When pets fall ill, timely and accurate diagnostics make all the difference. Our in-house diagnostic suite provides immediate clinical data, allowing our veterinary team to initiate targeted therapeutic interventions without delay.

  • ✓
    Comprehensive Consultations: In-depth investigation of gastrointestinal, respiratory, endocrine, and metabolic disorders.
  • ✓
    In-House Laboratory Testing: Complete blood count (CBC), serum chemistry, urinalysis, electrolytes, and cytology in 20 minutes.
  • ✓
    Digital Radiography (X-Ray): Crisp skeletal and thoracic imaging with minimal radiation exposure.
  • ✓
    Diagnostic Ultrasound: Non-invasive real-time evaluation of abdominal organs, bladder, kidneys, and cardiac chambers.

Advanced Clinical Care

Surgical & Dental Medicine

Led by Dr. James Okoye, our surgical and dental division provides state-of-the-art procedures under strict sterile protocols. Patient safety is guarded with multi-parameter anesthesia monitoring and dedicated nurse supervision.

  • ✓
    Veterinary Surgery: Spay/neuter, foreign body removal, mass removals, abdominal surgery, and orthopedic stabilization.
  • ✓
    Pre- & Post-Op Monitoring: Mandatory pre-anesthetic blood screenings and heated recovery bays with dedicated nursing.
  • ✓
    Dental Prophylaxis & Cleaning: Ultrasonic scaling above and below gum line, tooth polishing, and digital dental radiography.
  • ✓
    Tooth Extractions & Oral Surgery: Gentle surgical extractions with localized nerve blocks for pain-free recovery.
Note: Pre-surgical consultation and blood panel required prior to all elective surgical procedures.
