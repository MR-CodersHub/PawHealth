Our Founding Odyssey

Built From A Lifelong Devotion To Compassionate Animal Healing

PawHealth was founded in 2015 by Chief Veterinarian Dr. Sarah Mitchell with a clear mission: to create a hospital where medical precision, modern diagnostic technology, and heartfelt human empathy unite seamlessly.

What began as a close-knit community practice has blossomed into a full-service animal hospital serving over 5,000 animal families. Throughout our growth, our guiding philosophy has remained unchanged: we treat every companion with the exact same tenderness, dignity, and thoroughness we demand for our own pets.

“

We started PawHealth because no pet should ever feel terrified at the vet, and no pet parent should ever feel left in the dark about their companion's medical care.

— Dr. Sarah Mitchell, DVM, Chief Medical Officer & Founder
