Our Journey

A Lifelong Passion for Animal Healing

PawHealth was founded in 2015 by Chief Veterinarian Dr. Sarah Mitchell with an uncompromising vision: to establish an animal hospital where medical excellence, modern diagnostic technology, and genuine human empathy unite seamlessly.

What began as a close-knit community veterinary practice has blossomed into a full-service animal medical hospital serving over 5,000 registered animal patients. Throughout our growth, our foundational ethos has never wavered: we treat each patient with the same warmth, tenderness, and attentiveness we would expect for our own animal companions.

"Our mission is to safeguard animal health through proactive prevention, precise diagnostics, ethical treatments, and empowering pet parents with transparent medical knowledge."

Guiding Principles

Our Veterinary Care Philosophy

Four foundational values direct every decision we make in our examination rooms and surgical suites.

Compassion-First

Gentle, low-stress handling that calms anxious pets, minimizing fear and discomfort during every clinical exam.

Ethical Medicine

Evidence-backed clinical treatments with total transparency. We only recommend procedures that truly benefit your pet's wellbeing.

Personalized Plans

No cookie-cutter care. We tailor vaccination protocols, diet regimens, and senior care to your pet's lifestyle and biology.

Clear Communication

We break down complex diagnoses into understandable language and provide written estimates before any treatment begins.

State-of-the-Art Hospital

Our Clinic & Medical Facilities

Engineered specifically to minimize pet anxiety while providing modern diagnostic, surgical, and therapeutic capabilities.

Consultation Suites

Dedicated quiet rooms with non-slip exam tables, pheromone diffusers, and warm lighting to ease pet stress.

In-House Diagnostic Lab

Automated hematology, biochemistry, and urinalysis analyzers delivering vital blood work results in under 20 minutes.

Sterile Surgical Suite

Positive-pressure HEPA filtration, multiparameter vitals monitoring, and dedicated heated surgical recovery tables.

Digital Radiography & Ultrasound

Low-dose digital imaging that provides instant high-resolution skeletal, abdominal, and cardiac evaluations.

Separate Cat & Dog Wards

Sound-isolated recovery wards ensure feline patients do not see or hear dogs, speeding post-operative healing.

Welcoming Reception

Spacious check-in lounge with separated dog and cat waiting bays, complimentary treats, and warm client assistance.
