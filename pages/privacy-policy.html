How PawHealth Handles Your Information

This Privacy Policy explains how PawHealth Veterinary Clinic & Animal Hospital collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you visit our website, contact our clinic, or use our pet owner portal features.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal and pet-related information that you provide directly, including:

  • Contact details such as name, email address, and phone number
  • Pet details such as name, species, breed, age, and medical history notes
  • Appointment requests, service inquiries, and form submissions
  • Account or portal preferences when you register or sign in (demo features may use local browser storage)

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide and improve veterinary services, including to:

  • Respond to inquiries, schedule visits, and coordinate care
  • Maintain accurate pet health records and appointment history
  • Send important service-related communications and reminders
  • Improve website usability, safety, and client experience

3. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website may use cookies or browser local storage to remember preferences (such as theme and layout direction) and to support demo portal functionality. You can control browser storage through your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended if disabled.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only when necessary to operate our services, comply with legal obligations, protect safety, or with your consent (for example, referral coordination with another veterinary provider).

5. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of certain personal information. To make a request, please contact our clinic using the details below.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, contact PawHealth at hello@pawhealth.com or call (555) 123-4567.
