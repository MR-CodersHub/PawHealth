Daily Clinical Habits

Practical At-Home Wellness Habits

High-impact clinical habits recommended by veterinarians that significantly improve quality of life and longevity.

Annual Baseline Bloodwork

Pets mask pain and illness instinctively. Routine annual screenings catch early kidney changes and liver enzyme elevations years before symptoms manifest.

Maintain Lean Body Scoring

Obesity is the leading preventable cause of arthritis, diabetes, and shortened lifespan. Keeping dogs at an ideal body score can add up to 2.5 active years.

Digital Portal Record Access

Store up-to-date vaccine certificates, rabies tags, and microchip registration numbers securely in your phone portal for travel or emergency clinic visits.

Active Oral Hygiene Practice

Brush with enzymatic pet toothpaste weekly or use VOHC-approved dental chews. Preventing tartar stops oral bacteria from entering the bloodstream.
