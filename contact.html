Checklist for Pet Owners

Bringing the right items helps our veterinary doctors review past medical history accurately:

  • ✓ Past Health & Vaccine Records: Physical booklets or digital records from previous veterinary clinics.
  • ✓ Current Prescriptions: Medication bottles, supplements, or dietary formulas currently given.
  • ✓ Safe Transport: Secure cat carrier or well-fitted dog harness/leash.
  • ✓ Comfort Blanket or Treat: Familiar scents reduce animal anxiety in medical environments.
Helpful Answers

Contact & Appointment FAQs

Common queries regarding appointments, emergency protocols, and clinic procedures.

For routine wellness examinations, preventive vaccines, and dental checkups, we recommend booking 3 to 7 days in advance. For same-day sick animal concerns, call our urgent line early in the morning so we can triage your pet into our reserved urgent slots.

We accept walk-ins for true acute medical emergencies. However, to maintain low-stress consultation rooms and avoid long waiting room times for animals, all routine exams must be scheduled in advance.

In cases of severe trauma, collapse, difficulty breathing, toxic ingestion, or bloat, call our emergency line immediately at (555) 123-4568 before traveling. Calling ahead allows our veterinary surgical team to prepare oxygen, IV fluids, and the trauma bay before you arrive.

We accept all major credit and debit cards, contactless payments, and cash. We work with all leading pet health insurance providers and will gladly provide itemized medical receipts and direct electronic claim submissions.

You can request rescheduling or cancellation with one click inside your Pet Owner Dashboard under "My Appointments", or by calling our desk. We kindly request at least 24 hours notice so another pet in need can receive care.

Our standard appointments are 30 minutes — twice as long as generic veterinary clinics. This gives our doctors ample time to perform a gentle physical exam, discuss lifestyle habits, and answer all your questions without rushing.

Yes! We offer multi-pet family appointments in our larger family consultation suites. Please note in your booking enquiry that you have multiple animals so we can reserve an extended 60-minute consultation window.

All registered pet parents have 24/7 access to the Pet Owner Dashboard. Simply log in to view and print official vaccination certificates, medical summaries, and medication dosage records anytime.
